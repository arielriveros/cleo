# Valley

A 2 km mountain valley to fly through, with a day and night cycle: landscape layers for ground, rock, snow, forest floor, roads and trails; meadow grass, spruce forest, oaks about the hamlet and bushes made with the Foliage Creator; a torch-lit hamlet with enterable houses, a windmill, a campfire, a pond with floating crates, mist and god rays, all lit by baked GI volumes and light probes. Play opens a menu: a cinematic tour of the valley (made with the sequencer) or a free camera to fly.

A Cleo project folder: open it in the Cleo desktop app (Project ▸ Open Folder…), and what you change is what
git sees. See [examples/README.md](../README.md) for how the examples are kept: their binary data
(`npm run examples:fetch`), their tests (`npm test` here), and publishing a change.
