# Duskwell (the 2D metroidvania example)

A small 2D metroidvania, and the example the 2D tools are built against: one tilemap world split into rooms,
a warrior with a three-hit sword combo, bats, checkpoints, diamonds, and a dash relic that opens the way on.
Open it from the example gallery (**Duskwell**) and press Play.

It is generated by `tools/duskwell/build.ts` from the art in `build/platformer` (see
`tools/duskwell/README.md`); its scripts are `examples/scripts/Dusk*.ts`.

## Playing

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Move | A / D or the arrows | left stick or d-pad |
| Jump (hold for higher) | Space or K | A |
| Attack (again during a swing, or just after it, for the next of three) | J or left mouse | X |
| Dash (once found) | Left Shift or L | RB or B |
| Drop through a platform | Down + Jump | Down + A |

**The Gate** is where you wake: a step to hop, stone platforms you can jump up through and stand on, and four
diamonds. The lamp by the east door is a **checkpoint**: walk past it to light it, and you come back there,
whole, when you die. **The Long Hall** beyond has a Ruin Guard, two bats, a staircase of platforms up a
pillar, more diamonds, a second lamp, and a pit six cells wide that a running jump does not clear. The
glowing relic before the pit is the **dash**: jump, then dash in the air, to cross.

The **Ruin Guard** walks the hall floor, looking the way it walks. Once it sees you it chases: up the plank
and onto the pillar by jumping, down again by dropping, wherever you go. Close and level, it swings its
sword. Lose it and it searches where it last saw you, gives up after a few seconds, and walks back to its
watch. Its blade costs a heart; three sword hits kill it, and it drops two diamonds.

A bat hangs asleep until it sees you, then wakes and flies at you, round the pillar rather than through it,
and swoops when close. Its bite costs one of your five hearts and knocks you back, with a moment of
invulnerability after; three sword hits kill it, and it drops a diamond. Lose it and it flies home to roost.
Falling into the pit costs your whole health.

## How it is made

Everything in the scene is an ordinary node you can select and change:

- **Map** — one tilemap, 16 pixels a cell, cells sized one world unit. Its tileset marks the rock tiles
  **solid** and the stone slabs **one-way**; its **3D colliders** are off and its **sprite order** is
  **side view**. See [Tilemaps and 2D](tilemap-2d.md).
- **Rooms** — two **Room** nodes over the map. The camera keeps its view inside the room the player is in and
  slides across when the player walks through the door.
- **Camera** — a **Camera 2D** following `Player`, showing at least 480 × 270 art pixels, pixel-perfect,
  with a dead zone, look-ahead and a shake the player's script asks for on a hit.
- **Backdrop** — five **Parallax Layers**, from a sky that stays on screen to near woods that move with most
  of the camera's travel.
- **Player** — a **Character Body 2D** the engine walks and jumps, driven by its last child, a **Player
  Controller** reading Move and Jump, as a 3D Character is. Its **Sprite** carries the warrior's animation set
  (idle, run, jump, fall, three attacks, hurt, death) and a state machine that picks the movement clips from
  the body on its own; its **Sword** is an Area 2D the attack frames switch on and size. The `DuskPlayer`
  script is the game's own moves: the double jump, the dash, the wall slide and wall jump (each off until its
  pickup), the jump cut, and the combo, health, the hit, death and the respawn.
- **Enemies** — Character Bodies 2D, each driven by its last child, an **AI Controller** that runs an AI
  Brain asset (open them in the AI tab) and sees only the player's layer (its **perception layers**):
  - The **Ruin Guard** walks, on its **Guard Brain**: Patrol (the route **Hall Watch**), Chase (`path` to the
    player), Attack, Hurt, Search (`investigate`), Return (`path` home) and Dead. `DuskGuard` swings — the
    attack clip's frames switch its **Blade** Area 2D on — staggers, dies, and publishes the facts the
    brain reads: `attackReady`, `isAttacking`, `isHurt`, `isDead`, `targetDead`, `distanceFromHome`.
  - Each **bat** flies and passes through one-way platforms, on its **Bat Brain**: Sleep, Wake, Chase,
    Swoop, Hurt, Roost and Dead. `DuskBat` wakes it, owns the swoop's motion (`onMove2D`), and publishes
    `isAwake`, `swoopReady`, `isSwooping`, `isHurt`, `isDead`, `distanceFromHome`. Its **Bite** is an
    Area 2D the player's script reads `contactDamage` from.
- **Navigation** — two **Nav Meshes** in **2D** space over the whole map, baked from it: **Walker Nav**, a
  platform graph for the guard's body (its walks, the jumps up the plank and pillar, the drops and the
  drop-throughs), which also holds the guard's route; and **Flier Nav**, an air grid for a bat's. Each
  controller plans on the one that matches its body's movement, with no wiring. Change the map and bake
  again. See [Navigation in 2D](../scripting/ai.md#navigation-in-2d).
- **Pickups** and **Checkpoints** — Area 2Ds with `DuskPickup` (its `kind`: `diamond`, `heart`, `dash`,
  `doubleJump` or `wallJump`) and `DuskCheckpoint`.
- **HUD** — a UI root with three texts that `DuskHud` writes each frame.

The 2D scene draws through the flat 2D frame: no 3D passes, a fixed exposure, bloom only from the torches and
diamonds the tilesets' **overdrive** pushes past white.
